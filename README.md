# Bosa-Care

# Problem statement
~40% of Botswana's adult population are affected by cancer, diabetes, HIV\AIDS, anemia and hypertension, and require continuous care, but disconnected health services can make managing their conditions difficult leading to an increase in adult mortality rate.

# Solution statement
Bosa Care connects patients, doctors, pharmacies and healthcare services through an EMR and AI integrated system, enabling continuous monitoring, personalized treatment support, digital records and easier access to medication for better coordinated care
