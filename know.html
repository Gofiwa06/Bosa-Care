Cancer
Non-Contagious Chronic

Cancer is a broad group of diseases in which abnormal cells grow uncontrollably and can invade nearby tissue or spread (metastasise) to other parts of the body. There are over 100 distinct types, named for the organ or cell type where they start (e.g. breast, lung, colorectal, leukaemia). Outcomes vary enormously by type and stage at diagnosis — many cancers are highly treatable when caught early.

Common Symptoms
  • Unexplained weight loss and persistent fatigue
  • A new lump, thickening, or swelling anywhere on the body
  • Changes in a mole's size, shape, or colour
  • Persistent cough, hoarseness, or difficulty swallowing
  • Unusual bleeding or discharge (bowel, urine, vaginal)
  • Changes in bowel or bladder habits lasting more than a few weeks
  • Coughing up blood, sudden severe pain, or a rapidly growing lump — seek urgent medical review
  • Symptoms of a blocked bowel or airway are a medical emergency
Causes & Risk Factors
  • Accumulated mutations in genes controlling cell growth and division
  • Tobacco use is the single largest preventable cause worldwide
  • Chronic infections (HPV, hepatitis B/C, H. pylori) drive certain cancer types
  • Excess UV exposure, obesity, alcohol, and poor diet raise risk
  • Inherited gene mutations (e.g. BRCA1/2) increase risk for specific cancers
  • Age is the strongest overall risk factor — most cancers occur after 50
Management & Treatment
  • Surgery to remove localised tumours
  • Chemotherapy: systemic drugs that target rapidly dividing cells
  • Radiotherapy: targeted high-energy radiation to destroy cancer cells
  • Immunotherapy and targeted therapy for specific molecular profiles
  • Treatment plans are typically multidisciplinary and staged based on tumour type and spread
  • Palliative and supportive care manage symptoms and quality of life at any stage
⏱ Treatment length varies widely — months to years, sometimes lifelong monitoring
Prevention & Screening
  • Avoid tobacco in all forms; limit alcohol intake
  • Maintain a healthy weight and stay physically active
  • Use sun protection; avoid tanning beds
  • Get vaccinated against HPV and hepatitis B where recommended
  • Attend age-appropriate screening (mammograms, cervical smears, colonoscopy)
⚕ Medical Disclaimer: This information is for educational purposes only and does not replace professional medical advice. Cancer diagnosis and treatment must be guided by an oncologist based on individual test results.
